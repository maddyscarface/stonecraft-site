# StoneCraft creative library (Build 04 §6)

**Status:** working library. Every asset here is a *brief*. Humans approve claims, photos and publication. Claude/AI may draft copy and scripts; it may not invent jobs, reviews, results, ratings, durations or customer quotes.

## Ground rules: no fake proof
1. **Real or approved only.** Use StoneCraft job photos with a signed customer photo release, or licensed stock marked *Illustrative*. Vendor or manufacturer photos are never presented as StoneCraft jobs.
2. **Before/after must be the same surface, angle and light.** Simulated "before" images (docs/20) are labelled *Illustrative · simulated* and are never used in paid ads as proof.
3. **No unverified claims.** No "#1", "licensed & insured", years in business, review counts, "hospital-grade", "antimicrobial", "infection-control compliant", or "kills 99.9%". Healthcare work is described only as *scheduled around your infection-prevention team's requirements*.
4. **Treatment language is precise.** Always say which treatment is shown: *deep clean*, *grout colour seal*, *regrout*, *recaulk*, *hone/polish (natural stone only)*, *topical finish*. Never imply a glossy coating on glazed ceramic is the standard result.
5. **Brand distance.** Independent brand, never franchise language. No knights, armour, crowns, shields, "Sir"/royal motifs, or red/black/yellow palettes, so nothing reads as a Sir Grout look-alike. Palette: graphite #25292B, limestone #EEE9E1, champagne bronze #B89A70, white, sage-grey #77827C.
6. **Renters:** creative may invite renters to *request an assessment*. It must not imply a renter can authorise work. Copy: "Renting? We'll help you get approval from your landlord or property manager."
7. **Florida is pre-launch.** SFL creative says "now booking assessments / join the launch list" until the owner switches the market live. Never say "serving since…".

## Video scripts (15–30 s; captions burned in; no voice claims beyond the rules)

**V1 · C1 "One joint" (15 s, 9:16)**
- 0–3 s: Extreme macro of a discoloured grout joint. Text: *"Is it dirty… or is it the grout?"*
- 3–9 s: Brush and extraction pass, then the colour-seal applicator along the joint (real job only).
- 9–13 s: Slow slide reveal of the same frame after. Badge: *Real StoneCraft job · Boston* (or *Illustrative* for stock).
- 13–15 s: End card: *Restore the Surfaces You Love.* · **Get a Photo Assessment** · stonecraftpros.com

**V2 · C2 "The caulk line" (20 s, 4:5 / 9:16)**
- Old bead cut out (macro), surface cleaned and dried, tape lines, tooled bead, tape pulled. Text: *"Old caulk out. Clean lines in."* End card as V1.

## Photographer shot list (per real job; signed photo release first)
1. **Same tripod position before and after.** Mark the tripod feet with tape and record focal length, height and angle in the job notes.
2. Lighting: one neutral (5000 K) LED panel plus a raking-light pass for stone. Shoot the before and after under identical light.
3. Required frames per job:
   - Wide room
   - Mid (wall or floor section)
   - **Macro grout joint** (100 mm macro, f/8)
   - **Caulk line** (if in scope)
   - **Stone reflection** (raking light, if stone)
   - Process frames (hands and tools only)
   - Crew at work (faces only with a release)
   - Finished detail
4. Commercial:
   - Wide establishing shot with signage removed or blurred
   - Closure signage
   - Night timestamp
   - Floor macro
   - Clean exit at opening
5. Never shoot customer faces, house numbers, mail, family photos, patient areas or anything that identifies a patient or tenant.
6. File naming: `SC_<MKT>_<YYYYMMDD>_<JOBID>_<CATEGORY>_<BEFORE|AFTER|PROCESS>_<NN>.jpg`, e.g. `SC_BOS_20261115_J0142_C1_BEFORE_01.jpg`.

## CTA overlays (consistent system)
- **Type:** serif headline (site display face), sans label; limestone text on a graphite 85% bar, or graphite on limestone.
- **Primary residential:** "Get a Photo Assessment". **Commercial:** "Commercial Property Services" / "Request a Walkthrough".
- **Badges:** *Illustrative* (stock or simulated), *Real StoneCraft job · {City}* (released real job), *Pre-launch · Southeast Florida*.
- **Safe zones:** 9:16 keeps the top 14% and bottom 20% clear; 1:1 and 4:5 keep 8% margins.
- Logo bottom-right on the end card only. No phone number until the owner confirms one.

## Versioning
`SC-<MKT>-<CHANNEL>-<CATEGORY>-<FORMAT>-v<MAJOR>.<MINOR>-<YYYYMMDD>`, e.g. `SC-BOS-META-C1-916-v1.2-20261115`.
- **Major** = new footage or offer; **minor** = copy, crop or overlay tweak.
- Track each version in the creative sheet with these columns: asset id, source photo ids and release ids, claims used (each must be in docs/15 claims register), approver, approval date, live channels and retire date.
- The UTM `utm_content` = the asset id, so revenue reports roll up by creative version.
