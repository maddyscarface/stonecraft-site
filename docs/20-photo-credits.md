## Before/after slider pairs (Build 04 §1): illustrative

The home-page macro slider (`MacroCompare`, data in `src/config/photos.ts → beforeAfterPairs`) shows **one licensed stock photo per pair**. The "after" is the stock photo itself. The "before" is a **simulated** version of the same frame, made with `scripts/make-before-after.py` (grout grime via a joint mask, dulling, a yellow cast, light soap film, and etch rings on stone), so the camera angle matches exactly. Every pair carries an on-image **"Illustrative"** badge, the labels "Before · simulated" / "After", and a caption saying it is not a StoneCraft project. Replace these with real, permissioned StoneCraft job pairs (same angle, same lighting) as soon as they exist, and remove the badge only for real jobs.
